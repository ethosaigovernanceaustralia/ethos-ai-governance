Procurement Response Sprint

The questionnaire arrived. The deal is now a documentation exercise.

$2,990 [PRICE TBC] · 5 business days

Every Australian AI vendor selling into enterprise eventually receives one: a 60, 120, sometimes 200-question security and AI governance questionnaire, sent by a procurement team that will not book the next meeting until it comes back complete. The product has already impressed. The champion is already convinced. What stands between you and the contract is a spreadsheet asking for policies you have not written and evidence you have not assembled.

Most vendors respond in one of two ways. They answer thinly and hope, which experienced procurement reviewers detect immediately. Or they stall for weeks while the deal cools and the buyer's attention moves elsewhere.

There is a third option. The Procurement Response Sprint takes the live questionnaire off your desk and returns drafted responses, an assembled evidence pack and an honest map of your genuine gaps, in 5 business days.

Who it is for

  • check_circleAustralian AI vendors with a live enterprise security or AI governance questionnaire in hand and a real deal attached to it.
  • check_circleFounders and CTOs who could answer these questions eventually, but not this fortnight and not to procurement standard.
  • check_circleVendors with partial governance documentation who need it marshalled into the shape a buyer's risk team expects.

Who it is not for

  • remove_circleVendors with no questionnaire in play. If you want to be ready before one arrives, the Full Responsible AI Toolkit is the better instrument; this sprint exists for the live-fire situation.
  • remove_circleAnyone hoping to paper over gaps with confident-sounding answers. We draft accurately or not at all. Misrepresenting your governance posture to an enterprise buyer creates risk under the Australian Consumer Law's prohibitions on misleading conduct, and it is not something we will help with.
  • remove_circleOrganisations that merely use AI internally rather than building and selling it. That is outside the Ethos focus.

What you receive

  1. Drafted questionnaire responses. Every question answered in full, in your voice, grounded in what your organisation actually does. Where the honest answer is "not yet in place", the response is framed accurately alongside your remediation position.
  2. Assembled evidence pack. Your existing policies, certificates, architecture notes and governance documents collected, indexed against the questionnaire's evidence requests and packaged the way procurement reviewers expect to receive them.
  3. Gap register. A clear list of every question where your current posture falls short of what the buyer is asking for, with the shortfall stated plainly.
  4. Remediation shortlist. The gaps ranked by deal impact and effort to close, so you know which three things to fix this month and which can wait.

How it works

  1. Day 1: intake call. You share the questionnaire, the deal context and whatever documentation exists. One hour, then we take it from there.
  2. Day 2 to 4: drafting and assembly. Ethos drafts responses, assembles the evidence pack and builds the gap register. Short written queries to your team as needed; no further meetings required.
  3. Day 5: delivery and review call. Full response set delivered with a working session to review the drafts, confirm accuracy and agree the remediation shortlist. You submit; the deal moves.

Price and guarantee

$2,990 [PRICE TBC], fixed for one questionnaire of up to 150 questions. Larger instruments quoted on sight before you commit.

If we do not deliver the drafted responses, evidence pack and gap register within 5 business days of the intake call, we refund the purchase price in full. The guarantee is a refund of the fee paid. We do not and cannot guarantee that any buyer accepts your responses or that any deal closes; no honest adviser can.

Where this sits

The sprint is the done-with-you rung for procurement questionnaires. Below it sits the Procurement Readiness Guide (ETH-PRC-012), included with the Full Responsible AI Toolkit, which teaches you how enterprise buyers assess AI vendors and how to draft answers and build a reusable evidence pack yourself. The sprint is the escalation for a live deal with no time to learn. Above it, the Full Responsible AI Toolkit ($1,699) builds the governance documentation that makes the next questionnaire an afternoon's work rather than a rescue.
