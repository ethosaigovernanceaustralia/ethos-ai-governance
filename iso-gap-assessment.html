ISO 42001 Gap Assessment

Before you commit to certification, find out how far away it actually is.

$4,990 [PRICE TBC]

AS ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the AI management system standard, and it is beginning to appear in enterprise due diligence the way ISO 27001 did a decade ago. For an Australian AI vendor, certification is a genuine differentiator today precisely because so few organisations hold it.

But a certification pathway is a serious commitment of money, time and management attention. Entering one without knowing your starting position means either paying to discover gaps you could have mapped for a fraction of the cost, or stalling mid-pathway when the true scale of the work surfaces.

The ISO 42001 Gap Assessment is the disciplined first step: a formal assessment of your current AI management practices against every requirement of the standard, and a prioritised roadmap showing exactly what stands between you and a certifiable management system.

Who it is for

  • check_circleAustralian AI vendors seriously weighing ISO 42001 certification within the next 12 to 24 months and wanting the decision made on evidence rather than optimism.
  • check_circleVendors whose enterprise or government prospects have started referencing ISO 42001 in due diligence and who need a credible, board-ready account of where they stand.
  • check_circleOrganisations with some governance foundation in place, whether built with Ethos or elsewhere, ready to measure it against the international standard.

Who it is not for

  • remove_circleVendors with no governance documentation at all. The assessment will simply confirm that everything is a gap; the AU Compliance Core or Full Responsible AI Toolkit is the more useful spend at that stage.
  • remove_circleOrganisations looking for a certificate without a management system behind it. ISO 42001 certification is awarded by accredited certification bodies after independent audit; no consultant can grant it, and the assessment will not pretend otherwise.
  • remove_circleCompanies that use AI internally but do not build or sell it. Ethos serves AI vendors.

What you receive

  1. Formal gap assessment against AS ISO/IEC 42001:2023. Every clause and control of the standard assessed against your current documentation, practices and operating reality. Each requirement rated: met, partially met or not met, with the evidence basis recorded.
  2. Gap assessment report. A structured written report suitable for board and investor consumption: overall readiness position, clause-by-clause findings and the material risks in your current posture.
  3. Prioritised remediation roadmap. The gaps sequenced into a practical order of work, with effort estimates and dependencies, so the path from today's position to a certifiable management system is visible and plannable.
  4. Findings debrief. A working session to walk your leadership through the findings, pressure-test the roadmap against your commercial timeline and answer the question the assessment exists to answer: is certification the right move now, later or not yet.

If you want to gauge your position before committing to a formal assessment, the ISO 42001 Mapping Workbook (ETH-ISO-018, $699 [PRICE TBC]) is the self-serve starting point: an area-level mapping of the Ethos framework to the standard you complete yourself. The assessment goes where the workbook deliberately cannot, with pinpoint clause-by-clause and sub-control mapping against the licensed standard text.

How it works

  1. Intake and document collection. You supply existing policies, registers, procedures and records through a structured request list. We work with what genuinely exists, not what a template says should exist.
  2. Assessment interviews. Focused remote sessions with the people who own AI development, risk and operations, verifying that documented practice matches actual practice.
  3. Analysis and reporting. Ethos completes the clause-by-clause assessment and builds the report and roadmap.
  4. Debrief and decision. Findings presented, roadmap agreed and a clear-eyed recommendation given on timing for the certification pathway.

Typical elapsed time is three to four weeks depending on interview scheduling; the schedule is confirmed at intake.
